About

With our expertise and experience, we are simply at your service

Founded in 2006, the law firm of Me Ridha Ajmi offers personal and exclusive handling of entrusted cases. Each client is assisted and represented in the way most suited to defending their interests, respecting confidentiality, attorney-client privilege and the Swiss customs of the profession.

We also fully handle the administration of commercial companies, associations and non-profit foundations, active in Switzerland and abroad.

  • The firm handles matters in French, English and Arabic.
  • A human-scale firm providing personalized case management.
  • Bar admission in Geneva, obtained in 2006.
